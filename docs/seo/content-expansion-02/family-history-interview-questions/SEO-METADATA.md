## Snippet answer candidate

Good family history interview questions are short, open-ended, and specific enough to invite a scene rather than a yes-or-no answer. Before recording, agree on the purpose, audience, privacy boundaries, and review process. Start with childhood, people, places, traditions, turning points, and what the narrator wants future relatives to understand.
