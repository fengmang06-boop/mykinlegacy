## Exclusions

- Do not retarget the keepsake guide to interview questions.
- Do not expand the preservation candidate into another question list.
- Do not describe an interview as verified genealogy.
- Do not target official heraldry, surname coat of arms, ancestry DNA, obituary writing, dementia care, therapy, or legal testimony.
- Do not create separate thin pages for parents, grandparents, mothers, fathers, or reunions until first-party query evidence justifies them.
