# Gemstone and Crystal Framework

Allowed only when explicitly selected, linked to a person/date/story, or clearly
described as a visual material choice.

Permitted language: “traditionally associated with,” “personally meaningful,”
“selected by the family,” “used here to represent,” and “chosen as a visual
reminder of.”

Forbidden claims include guaranteed protection, healing, destiny change,
wealth attraction, medical efficacy, or scientifically proven energy.

Birthstones, memorial stones, diamond-like commitment, sapphire-like loyalty,
emerald-like continuity, ruby-like devotion, and amethyst-like remembrance are
optional prompts—not default meanings. The customer’s stated meaning prevails.
