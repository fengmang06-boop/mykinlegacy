# Crest Differentiation QA

Automated and founder review fields:

- overall similarity score
- dominant-symbol uniqueness
- silhouette uniqueness
- frame/composition uniqueness
- color/material uniqueness
- evidence coverage
- unsupported-symbol count
- template-reuse warning

Pass requirements:

- At least four structural dimensions differ from every comparison example.
- Unsupported-symbol count is zero.
- Every major element maps to an evidence source.
- Primary silhouettes remain distinct in grayscale.
- Removing color still leaves recognizably different compositions.
- No royal, military, or official-arms implication unless the content is merely
  explaining the non-heraldry boundary.
