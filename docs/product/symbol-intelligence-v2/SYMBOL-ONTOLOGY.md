# Symbol Ontology V2

Every candidate symbol has:

- concept: guidance, continuity, remembrance, resilience, devotion, craft, etc.
- form family: botanical, geographic, celestial, object, abstract, material.
- source: `FAMILY_EVIDENCE`, `USER_EXPLICIT_PREFERENCE`,
  `OCCASION_CONTEXT`, `CULTURAL_REFERENCE`, `VISUAL_SUPPORT_ONLY`, or
  `NOT_JUSTIFIED`.
- evidence reference and confidence.
- cultural/privacy constraints.
- inclusion and exclusion rationale.
- composition role: primary, supporting, border, material, or negative space.

`NOT_JUSTIFIED` candidates cannot enter a design. Symbols are contemporary
artistic language, not official heraldic entitlement or ancestry proof.
