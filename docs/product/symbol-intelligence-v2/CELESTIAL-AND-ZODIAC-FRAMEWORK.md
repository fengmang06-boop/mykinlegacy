# Celestial and Zodiac Framework

Constellations, zodiac signs, birth sky, guiding stars, moon phases, and family
members represented as stars require explicit opt-in and a stated relationship
to birth, remembrance, direction, belief, or shared story.

The experience must not predict destiny, infer belief, or assume astrology is
accepted. Celestial elements remain subordinate to real family evidence.
Descriptions identify them as personal or artistic symbolism, never scientific
causation.
