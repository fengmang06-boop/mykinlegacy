# Brand Clarity and Trust Audit

## Current weaknesses

- Repeated dark crest treatments make the offer resemble a crest commodity.
- Layered document previews obscure deliverables and reduce trust.
- The five-step intake understates the depth of personalization.
- Meaning, evidence, privacy, digital delivery, and non-heraldry boundaries are
  present but fragmented.

## V2 clarity contract

Within five seconds, users must understand that MyKinLegacy turns real family
stories, people, places, values, and milestones into contemporary symbolic art
plus a private five-part digital collection for USD $49.

Trust content must state: founder reviewed, private by default, evidence-led,
customer-controlled sensitive inputs, clear review process, digital delivery,
not physical shipping, not official arms, not genealogy research, and no
fabricated ancestry. No invented testimonials or quantities.
