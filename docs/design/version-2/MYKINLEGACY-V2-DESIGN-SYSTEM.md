# MyKinLegacy Version 2 Design System

## Brand direction

Premium modern family archive; intimate, emotionally rich, evidence-led,
trustworthy, warm, artistic, and contemporary. Avoid royal, military, wedding
template, SaaS-card, and black-gold merchandise signals.

## Core tokens

- Forest: `#173C32`; deep forest: `#102D27`
- Archival ivory: `#F7F0E2`; parchment: `#E9D8BB`
- Charcoal: `#262A28`; readable muted ink: `#5A5B55`
- Antique brass: `#9A7443`; bronze: `#7C5B3B`
- Restrained burgundy: `#743D43`
- Mineral accents are optional and evidence-linked.
- Display: editorial serif; narrative: readable serif; UI: clean sans.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96.
- Containers: 72rem content, 88rem visual; mobile gutter 20px.
- Radius: 8px documents, 16px panels, 999px only for compact status pills.
- Borders: warm gray/brass at low contrast; never arbitrary bright gold.
- Shadows: soft archival elevation, no heavy black glow.
- Focus: 3px mineral-blue outline with 2px offset.
- Motion: 160–240ms; disabled or simplified for reduced motion.
- Breakpoints: 360, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1440.

Images use intrinsic ratios and `object-fit: contain`. Text and artwork never
overlap. Deep backgrounds require ivory text meeting WCAG AA.
